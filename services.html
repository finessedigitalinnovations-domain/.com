Questions we often get

Can I see a design before I choose it?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your type of business and we will send demo links you can open on your phone.

Can you change the colours and layout?

Yes. Colours, fonts, sections, text and images can all be changed to suit your business.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, your logo if you have one, the colours you like, photos of your products or place, and the WhatsApp number and email customers should use. If you do not have some of these yet, tell us and we will start with what you have.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the design and how much needs to change. Message us with your business type and we will give you a price.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You tell us what you want and we handle the building.

Found a design you like?

Send us a message and we will take it from there.
