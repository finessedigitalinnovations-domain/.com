FINESSE DIGITAL INNOVATIONS - BUSINESS & CORPORATE TEMPLATES
===========================================

How to view
-----------
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in any web browser.
3. Click any template to open it. Each template is a single HTML file.

Good to know
------------
- Every template works on phones, tablets and computers, and follows light/dark mode.
- All names, prices, reviews and photos are sample content. Photos are drawn in code.
