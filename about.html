A small studio building websites for small businesses.

Your Goal is Our Goal.

It is how we judge our own work: if customers can find you and contact you easily, the website is doing its job.

Finesse Digital Innovations was founded in 2025 by Felix. Felix works in front-end development and design, and noticed that many local businesses either had no website or had one that was hard to use on a phone.

We decided to do one thing well. We give small businesses a proper website, fitted to their brand and ready to share with customers, without the cost and wait of building from scratch.

Most of our customers reach businesses through WhatsApp, so our websites are built around that. A visitor taps a button, a chat opens with your number, and the conversation starts.

Who we build for

Shops

Clothes, electronics, groceries, beauty, jewellery and handmade goods, sold online with clear product pages and a simple way to order.

Food businesses

Restaurants, cafes, bakeries and caterers who need a menu, opening hours and an easy way to take orders and bookings.

Companies and firms

Agencies, consultants, law and accounting firms, builders and service companies who need to look credible online.

What you can expect

Plain language, without jargon. You see the website before it goes live. You can ask for changes, and we tell you clearly what you are getting.
